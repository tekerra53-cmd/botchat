This handbook summarizes key student information for the University of Lagos for the 2026/2027 session. It covers programs, admissions, fees, housing, library services, grading, and student affairs.

### Management Sciences Catalog Snapshot

- O'Level upload deadline: August 19, 2026

Students should always confirm final dates on the official portal and departmental notices.

## Admission Requirements
- UTME benchmark: minimum score of 200
- First choice: University of Lagos must be selected as first choice
- O'Level: at least five credits including English Language and Mathematics in one sitting
- Age requirement: at least 16 years old by September 30, 2026
- Result upload: WAEC, NECO, or GCE results must be uploaded to both JAMB CAPS and the UNILAG portal by August 19, 2026

## Fees and Payment

### Summary of Obligatory Undergraduate Fees
- Acceptance charges: approximately N45,000 for fresh students
- Faculty of Arts baseline: roughly N51,500 for first-year levies and tuition
- Science and lab-based courses: up to N222,500 inclusive of laboratory services and professional fees

### Payment Method
- Payment is electronic only through Remita on the student portal
- Cash payment is not accepted

## Hostel Accommodation

### Policy
- Hostel bed spaces are non-transferable
- Selling, buying, or squatting in a bed space is prohibited
- Violations may lead to expulsion from the hall and suspension from the university

### Allocation
- Priority is given to freshmen and final-year students
- Allocation is done through online balloting on the student portal
- Payment must be completed within 3 to 5 days after provisional allocation or the space will be forfeited

### Prohibited Activities
- Squatting or subletting
- Heavy electrical appliances such as hot plates, boiling rings, kettles, and washing machines
- Cooking inside standard bedrooms
- Unauthorized room modifications or changing locks
- Defacing walls with paint or posters

### Curfew
- Most halls enforce a night curfew, usually around 10:00 PM or 11:00 PM

### Hostel Fees
- General hostel bed spaces: N80,000 per session
- Sodeinde Hall: N250,000 per session
- Moremi Hall Extension: N500,000 per session
- Landmark Student Hostel: up to N710,000 for shared units and N950,000 for single rooms

## Library Services

### Opening Hours
- Term time: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
- Vacation period: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Faculty and departmental libraries: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- Exam period: 24-hour reading service may be available at the Akintunde Ojo Library Basement

### Access Rules
- Valid university ID card is required
- Large bags and coats must be checked at the baggage counter
- External researchers may need an introduction letter, photo ID, and visitor registration

### Conduct Rules
- Silence must be observed
- Food and colored drinks are not allowed in reading areas
- Seat reservation is prohibited
- Group work should take place in designated zones

## Grading and Assessment

### CGPA Scale
- 70 to 100: A, 5.00, First Class
- 60 to 69: B, 4.00, Second Class Upper
- 50 to 59: C, 3.00, Second Class Lower
- 45 to 49: D, 2.00, Third Class
- 40 to 44: E, 1.00, Pass
- 0 to 39: F, 0.00, Fail

### Assessment Structure
- Continuous assessment: 30% or 40%
- Final examination: 60% or 70%

### Exam Rules
- Minimum attendance requirement: 65%
- Phones, smartwatches, and programmable calculators are not allowed in the exam hall
- Late arrival beyond 30 minutes may result in denial of entry
- Students may not leave during the first hour or the final 15 minutes
